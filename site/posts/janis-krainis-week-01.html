The True Cost of Email Chaos in Freight Forwarding

Janis Krainis · 2026-09-25

During my upcoming internship at Drewes, one of the most obvious operational bottlenecks stood out immediately: manual email management and endless forwarding chaos. In the fast-paced world of European logistics, communication is everything, yet a huge chunk of the working day is wasted simply sorting, routing, and forwarding emails between departments and clients.

The Problem: Where Time and Money Go to Die

Every time an operations manager has to manually open an email, figure out which forwarder handles the cargo, and re-route the message, the company loses precious minutes. Across an entire team and hundreds of daily shipments, this friction compounds into massive losses in productivity.

  • The Time Sink: Hours spent daily on manual message triage instead of high-value tasks.
  • Human Error: Misrouted emails lead to delayed responses and missed quotes.
  • Financial Impact: In freight forwarding, a delayed reply often means lost business to a faster competitor.

Looking Ahead

Over the next 12 weeks of this portfolio, I will explore how automation and modern AI tools can tackle this communication breakdown. The goal is not just theoretical—it is to look at how Drewes and similar logistics providers can eliminate back-office drag and turn chaotic inboxes into streamlined pipelines.
