1,400 Better Hours: A Business Case for the Coach Hub

Brecht Masschaele · 2026-10-25

Hook

Last week my FOMO Check gave the Coach Hub a "pilot" verdict. A pilot still needs a price tag, and "it saves coaches time" is not a business case. So I built a second skill, the AI Value Audit, and ran the Coach Hub through it. Here are the numbers. 📊

The Candidate

AI support for BAP coaches. Our coaches guide up to five graduation students each, next to their teaching. Every year that's 250 students (70 in semester 1, 180 in semester 2), 40 coaches and 21 paid hours per student: 5,250 hours.

In a programme's value chain, the BAP is a primary activity: it's what students graduate on. Coaching it well is core work, not support.

The Coach Hub bundles several jobs. The 5-criterion filter split them. Five jobs passed (repetitive, pattern-based, error-tolerant, cost-justifiable, verifiable): Research Plan feedback, a Socratic coach, a feedback checker, a source checker and draft emails. To-do lists, overviews and coordinator monitoring failed on one point: they follow fixed dates. That's rules, not AI. They're out of the case.

The Numbers

Assumptions:

  • Loaded rate: €68,000 (10-year lecturer gross salary) × 1.3 ÷ 1,600 h = €55.25/h
  • AI success rate: 80% (est.), not tested yet
  • Seats: Team plan, $20 × 0.92 = €18.40/month (claude.com, 19 October)

Time per student (runs per student, minutes now → review with AI):

Tool Runs Now With AI
Research Plan feedback 2 60 15
Socratic coach 10 30 10
Feedback checker 2 60 15
Source checker 3 30 10
Draft emails 5 10 3
Total 22 680 205

A failed output costs extra: the coach reviews it and still does the task.

The tools already exist, so there's no build cost. Review time is already inside the time-saved figure.

Net benefit: €78,041 − €11,484 = €66,557 in Year 1, €68,767 from Year 2.
Payback: €2,210 one-time ÷ (€68,767 ÷ 12) = 0.4 months.

The honest caveat: 1,413 hours is capacity, not cash. No salary goes down. That's fine, because it's the goal: less admin and first drafts, more in-depth feedback and coaching sessions.

The Pressure Test

Benefits 50% too optimistic? Year 1 still nets €27,536.

Salary range: a starting lecturer gives €49,927 net; a senior one €90,947. With the course's ×1.4 multiplier, net rises to €72,356.

Break-even: the AI must be usable in just 37% of runs.

The money survives every test. The risk is elsewhere. A fabricated error, like "the student implemented your feedback" or "this source is fake", can lead to an unfair grade or a fraud accusation. That cost isn't in my table. The mitigation is a rule: AI drafts, the coach decides.

The Insight

The case isn't fragile on money, it's fragile on one assumption: that coaches today spend 11.3 of their 21 hours on these five tasks. The Socratic coach alone (10 × 30 minutes) drives most of the gain. If that number is off, the case shrinks fast.

So the pilot measures time, not just quality. Five coaches log their hours for six weeks in semester 2, after a capability test on the source and feedback checkers and a DPA review. Go to all 40 coaches if at least 60% of outputs are usable and review times match the estimates.

At about €8 per freed hour against €55.25 for what that hour costs, the question isn't whether we can afford it. It's whether we can prove the hours are real. 🎓
