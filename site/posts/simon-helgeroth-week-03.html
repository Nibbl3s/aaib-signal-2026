AI in Recruitment: Real Value or FOMO? Deconstructing Workable's AI claims

Recruitment is a branch consisting of a lot of AI vendors and also one of the more sensitive ones. The tools can decide who gets seen and who gets screened out and may risk unfair evaluations of candidates.

The vendor in this case is Workable who claim to sell an agent that helps you by defining roles, reaching out to candidates, and screening and scoring profiles, while keeping you in control at every step.

Claims Table

# Claim (quote or paraphrase) Section Tag (Fact/Aspiration/Avoidance) Your Reasoning
1 An agentic recruiting platform, built for real ROI Rubric Aspiration/Avoidance It promises ROI which can be an expectation of its design. But does not specify timeframe or %.
2 It produces an optimized, ready-to-use job description, hiring criteria, and interview questions. How it fits Aspiration Optimized and Ready to use are subjective.
3 The agent searches the talent market, evaluates profiles against your job needs, sends personalized outreach, follows up to gather missing details, and advances candidates through your pipeline. How it fits Fact It is verifiable during the demo and does not state numbers on how well it will perform.
4 It already powers sourcing, screening, and candidate communication for thousands of teams worldwide. Already powering Avoidance While they present companies that use Workable they don't specify which features.
5 Explore how it can transform your hiring workflow Demo-box Aspiration Transform is both subjective and a promise.
6 You stay in control while the agent handles time-intensive tasks, so you can spend more time on human decisions How it fits Avoidance Very vague. How much time is saved? What is time-intensive?

Claims to Deconstruct

  • #1 An agentic recruiting platform, built for real ROI.
    This is both an aspiration and an avoidance. Return of investment is a promise of value but what does "real" even mean? Further they dodge specifics such as within what timeframe and on a percentage return.
  • #3 The agent searches the talent market, evaluates profiles against your job needs, sends personalized outreach, follows up to gather missing details, and advances candidates through your pipeline.
    As they offer a trial these actions become verifiable.
  • #4 It already powers sourcing, screening, and candidate communication for thousands of teams worldwide.
    While they do name some companies on their website they don’t specify what kind of features they use. Is it the AI agent or just Workable as a platform? I therefore view it as avoidance.

FOMO Framework Application

Q1: Specific problem?
Workable explicitly says that "Workable's Agent helps you by defining roles, reaching out to candidates, and screening and scoring profiles, while keeping you in control at every step." But the platform still requires human control at every step and we don’t get any information about how these things are regarded as a problem today and what they actually solve. We also don’t get any measurable metric on how it can reduce time etc.

Q3: Can we verify?
Well, you can run a 15-day free trial with your own data which enables assessments on whether it can save you time and how it might perform certain tasks.

Q4: What's the total cost?
Workable provides different pricing depending on model and on how many coworkers there are. However, a lot of details are not mentioned, and they write "unlimited active jobs" with a box next to it that says "subject to fair using limits" which is difficult to interpret. Further for the agent it says 3000 free credits but do not specify the costs afterwards.

Verdict: Pilot/Trial.
Since they offer a 15-day free trial where you can upload own data and verify claims and ideas you have without paying I would try it. However, as previously stated, there is no clearly defined business problem and the pricing contains of unclear limits. Therefore, I would be careful in buying the product without a good set of test data during the trial and the concrete specifics on the price before thinking about moving forward.
