Can AI Find Odoo? A GEO Audit

When a company wants ERP software, it can search Google or ask ChatGPT for recommendations. This made me wonder whether companies need to change how they market themselves. If AI doesn't recommend your company, potential customers might never discover it.

The Company

I chose Odoo because it is a Belgian ERP software company and directly connects to my beat, AI in ERP and Business Process Automation. Odoo offers accounting, CRM, inventory and other business applications. I am also interested in using Odoo for my own projects, so I wanted to see how discoverable it is.

My GEO Audit

I used the six criteria from our Week 5 framework, scoring each from 0 to 3.

Criterion Score Evidence
Structured Data 1/3 I inspected Odoo's homepage source code and found application/ld+json with Schema.org markup. It identifies Odoo as an OnlineStore and includes its name, logo and social profiles.
Content Parseability 2/3 Odoo's website separates applications into clear categories, including CRM, Finance and Inventory.
Third-Party Mentions 3/3 Odoo appears on independent software review websites such as G2 and Capterra.
AI Citation Presence 3/3 I asked ChatGPT twice to recommend ERP software for a Belgian SME. Odoo appeared at the top both times.
Authority Signals 2/3 Odoo publishes product information and customer examples, although I haven't fully verified expert authorship.
Brand Direct-Search 2/3 Odoo appeared as the first non-sponsored Google result when I searched its name. I haven't checked every additional brand signal.
Total 13/18 AI-Present

The strongest finding was that ChatGPT recommended Odoo in both of my tests. However, two searches are not enough to prove that it always appears first.

Vulnerability Assessment

I also assessed Odoo using the four vulnerability factors.

Factor Risk Reason
Visit Dependency Medium Website visits help attract customers, but Odoo earns revenue from software services and subscriptions.
AI Substitutability Low AI can explain ERP software, but cannot replace Odoo's actual applications.
Intermediary Position Low Odoo provides software directly instead of acting as a comparison website.
SEO Asset Weight Medium Search visibility helps Odoo, but its products and brand also create value.

My Verdict

With a score of 13/18, I would classify Odoo as AI-Present according to our course framework.

I think Odoo is in a relatively strong position because it has a clear website, third-party mentions and visibility in my ChatGPT tests.

Its biggest improvement opportunity is to make more detailed product information easy for AI systems to understand and cite.

I would also test different AI assistants and more specific questions before claiming that Odoo has strong GEO performance overall.

What I Learned

Before this week, I mainly thought about Google rankings when discussing online visibility. Now I understand that AI assistants can influence which companies customers consider.

For smaller ERP providers, this could become a serious challenge. Even if their software is good, they might struggle to compete if AI assistants mostly recommend established brands.

My next question is whether smaller Belgian ERP companies can improve their visibility enough to compete with Odoo.
