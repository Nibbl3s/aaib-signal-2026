The €0.0005 Campaign: What One AI Marketing Generation Actually Costs

Ksenija Maruscaka · 2026-09-25


Marketing agencies love talking about "endless personalized campaigns at scale." Nobody ever asks what one generated copy or promo email actually costs, because the agency invoice just says "AI platform fee" and the total has a comma in it.

This week I learned to read the actual unit: tokens. Not words — tokens, roughly ¾ of an English word, and the difference matters a lot when you scale personalization across multiple languages in Europe.

The numbers

A standard personalized marketing email or ad variant is about 200 tokens in (customer data, product catalog) and 400 tokens out (generated copy). At a standard commercial tier ($2.00 per 1M input, $12.00 per 1M output — based on the course pricing reference):

  • Input: 200 ÷ 1,000,000 × $2.00 = $0.0004

  • Output: 400 ÷ 1,000,000 × $12.00 = $0.0048

  • One personalized message: about $0.0052.

The insight

Thirty thousand generated messages a month is roughly $156 of tokens. The raw AI bill was never the real problem — which means the hefty monthly subscription fee for "AI marketing automation" was mostly paying for the wrapper, interface, and vendor margin, while nobody looked at the error risks. That is the actual finding: the token cost is trivial; the wrapper and the cost of automated brand errors are where the budget bleeds. Week 3 is about reading vendor claims, and I am now deeply suspicious of every marketing pitch that promises "cheap AI."

What I want to learn next

Whether the multilingual tax (French, Dutch, or German costing significantly more tokens than English) quietly breaks marketing budgets for cross-border European retail campaigns — and whether any brand actually calculates it before launching.

One number I want before Week 2: what companies actually spend on human copyeditors fixing AI hallucinations in marketing copy. If anyone has real data from their marketing experience, bring it to class.
