AI Vendor Actually Replies to Pricing Email; Experts Suspect Hallucination

The reply contained no number, two exclamation marks and one calendar link.

By The Noise Staff (one unpaid intern)

GHENT — In what analysts are calling "statistically impossible," compliance-AI vendor ComplyMaxx has reportedly replied to a pricing enquiry sent by a student, just 41 business days after it was sent.

The reply, which arrived at 03:14 on a Sunday, reads in full: "Thank you for your interest!! Pricing depends on your needs. Let's hop on a quick call."

Researchers confirmed the email contained no price, no range and no number of any kind, apart from the 15 in "15-minute discovery call."

"We ran it through three different models. Two classified it as a genuine reply. The third classified it as a haiku."

The student, who asked to remain anonymous and is definitely not the author of this site's Week 1 post, described the moment of opening the email as "emotional."

"I asked for a price. I received a calendar link. This is the closest I have come to a number in six weeks."

ComplyMaxx did not respond to our request for comment, which experts say is "much more on brand."

At press time, the ComplyMaxx website still promised "transparent pricing," a phrase our Claim Gauntlet classifies as an Aspiration.
