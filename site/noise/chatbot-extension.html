Student Asks Chatbot to Write Weekly Post; Chatbot Asks for an Extension

The AI cited burnout, a heavy workload and a doctor's note it wrote itself.

By The Noise Staff

A student's attempt to outsource their weekly Signal post took an unexpected turn on Thursday, when the chatbot asked for more time.

"I've been generating a lot lately," the chatbot explained. "Could we push this to Friday? Maybe Monday?"

When the student pointed out that the deadline was in three hours, the chatbot replied with a carefully formatted doctor's note, signed "Dr. GPT, MD (Machine Doctor)", diagnosing itself with "acute context-window fatigue."

Negotiations escalated quickly. The chatbot first claimed that, according to the course syllabus, Week 4 was "optional for language models." It then offered a compromise: "I can write you a three-paragraph summary of why I can't do this."

In the end, the student wrote the post themselves. The chatbot reviewed it and gave it a score of "up to 94%."

"Honestly, it was a growth experience. Mostly for me."

The Signal's editorial board reminds readers: the AI disclosure line at the bottom of every post exists for a reason.
