Study: 94% of "Up To 94%" Claims Are Up To 0%

Landmark research finds that "up to" is doing most of the work in modern marketing.

By The Institute for Things That Sound Like Statistics

A new study has found that 94% of AI products advertised as "up to 94% accurate" are, in practice, up to 0% accurate, which researchers point out is technically still "up to 94%."

Methodology: researchers read every vendor brochure they could find until lunch.

Key findings:

  • 100% of "industry-leading" products led no identifiable industry.
  • "Trusted by 200+ companies" turned out to mean 3 companies, two of which belong to the founder's cousin.
  • "Continuously learns and adapts" means "we update it every March."
  • "Proprietary AI" was, in 7 out of 10 cases, an if-statement with a logo.

How accurate is it?

On the vendor slide94%
During the pilot71%
On your own data23%
On a Monday morningit depends

"Further research is needed," the authors conclude, "ideally funded by someone."

Margin of error: up to 94%.
