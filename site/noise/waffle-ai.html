Company Replaces Spreadsheet With €500,000 AI; AI Turns Out to Be a Spreadsheet

"Technically, a spreadsheet is a neural network with one layer and strong opinions," says vendor.

By The Noise Business Desk

A mid-sized Flemish waffle wholesaler, WaffleCorp, has discovered that the €500,000 "NeuroDemand Forecaster™" it bought last year is, at its core, an Excel file from 2016.

The discovery was made by an intern who opened the system's "proprietary neural core" and found a single tab named Sheet1 containing the formula =AVERAGE(B2:B13).

"We paid half a million euros for an average," the CFO said. "Of twelve months."

The vendor defended the product. "Technically, a spreadsheet is a neural network with one layer and strong opinions," a spokesperson said, before describing the formula as "deep learning, but shallow."

Meanwhile, the old forecasting spreadsheet, built in 2011 by Marleen from accounting, turned out to be 3% more accurate. Marleen has been offered a consulting contract worth €500,000.

At press time, WaffleCorp had renewed its NeuroDemand contract for three more years, citing "switching costs."
