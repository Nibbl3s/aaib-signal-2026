\## Top 5 avoidances



\### 1. What exactly is the AI?



The proposal repeatedly mentions proprietary ML and LLM technology but does not explain the model architecture, what the models actually do, or where AI is necessary.



\### 2. What does “94% accuracy” actually mean?



The proposal never defines the metric, benchmark, dataset, prediction horizon, baseline or error rate. A 94% figure is meaningless without this context.



\### 3. Where is the independent evidence?



The proposal refers to 200+ customers and client self-reporting but provides no named references, independent case studies or independently measured results.



\### 4. What does integration actually require?



“Works with existing systems” is not enough. The proposal does not specify supported systems, implementation work, data preparation, integration costs or technical requirements.



\### 5. What is the real total cost?



The €252,000 annual license does not necessarily represent the full cost. Internal staff time, integration, data preparation, support, API/LLM costs, maintenance and switching costs need to be included.
