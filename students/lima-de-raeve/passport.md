## Checkpoint 2 — Week 8

### My Build

**What can my tool do now?**

My AI customer service classifier can categorise multilingual customer enquiries into Automate, Human Intervention and Insufficient Information. Following a prompt revision, it better distinguishes between incomplete requests and identifiable complaints or financial claims requiring human intervention.

**What can't it do yet?**

It cannot independently manage customer service, retrieve verified order information or respond to customers without further integration. Its reliability still needs to be validated through a supervised pilot with real customer enquiries.

**Which number proves it?**

After revising the classification prompt, agreement with expected classifications increased from 80% to 100% across ten challenging customer messages, representing an improvement of 20 percentage points.

### Reflection

At the beginning of this course, I mainly considered AI a tool that could automate tasks and increase productivity. I now understand that implementing AI in a business requires much more consideration of reliability, financial value, integration, risks and employee acceptance. I have also learned that a successful experiment does not automatically justify full implementation and that human oversight and continuous evaluation remain essential.
