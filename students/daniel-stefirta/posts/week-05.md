# AI Can Find Room 8 Studio. That Does Not Guarantee Its Place.

A game-art supplier can lose a potential client before anyone visits its portfolio. If a developer asks an AI assistant for three outsourcing options, a missing studio never reaches the shortlist. The risk is exclusion from consideration, not simply lower website traffic.

I chose [Room 8 Studio](https://room8studio.com/), Room 8 Group's dedicated art-production team. Its services include concepts, 3D environments, characters, animation and UI/UX for games. Unlike a studio that primarily develops its own titles, it explicitly offers services another developer can buy. That makes “would AI recommend this provider?” a meaningful question. My audit concerns Studio art services, not the parent group's entire offering.

Runable collected the website evidence and ran four fresh, logged-out ChatGPT conversations with web search on 23 October 2026. No prompt named Room 8. The underlying model was not disclosed. This was agent-operated research, not testing I personally performed.

## The scorecard

|---|---:|---|
| Structured data | 1/3 | Three sampled pages contain WebPage and WebSite schema, but no Organization or Service entities. This is partial credit for generic markup, not a claim that Organization schema exists. |

| Third-party mentions | 2/3 | Two exact-brand Google result pages exposed nine non-company-controlled domains. [80.lv](https://80.lv/partners/room-8-studio) describes “Room 8 Group's Art team”. Profiles establish visibility, not independent quality verification. |

| Authority signals | 2/3 | The [company page](https://room8studio.com/company/) names “Maksim Makovsky”, “3D Art Director”, lists credits and claims “ISO 27001”. Case evidence exists, but the sampled case article lacks a named studio-author byline. |
| Brand direct-search | 2/3 | Its official site was Google's first organic result, with sitelinks. Wikipedia covered Room 8 Group, not a separate Studio entity. |

**Total: 12/18, AI-Present.**

## The verdict

AI did recommend Room 8 in these tests. However, repeating the identical broad query changed the third supplier from Keywords Studios to Art Bully Productions. Room 8's inclusion was stable; the shortlist was not. One run required a connection-error retry. Four completed responses do not establish universal visibility or independently verified supplier performance.

My priority improvement is accurate Organization and Service schema distinguishing Studio from Group. This is a testable hypothesis about clearer identification, not proof that markup causes recommendations. Room 8 already surfaced despite that gap.

## The mechanism

My vulnerability assessment is provisionally moderate: AI can summarize supplier information, but cannot replace inspecting original work, obtaining a quotation or validating delivery capability through a recommendation alone. Production contracts are not page views. A traffic decline could coexist with qualified AI-originated enquiries, or conceal fewer future contracts. Without channel-attribution data, the revenue effect is unknown.

This single-company audit cannot show whether most game-art suppliers are prepared. It does show why discoverability and procurement quality need separate tests.

Would Room 8 still appear when the buyer specifies a small budget, a tight deadline and a particular engine pipeline?
