### Looking Up Two Companies: ML6 and EY

Both companies make their AI-related services visible online, but they position themselves differently. ML6 presents itself as a specialist AI engineering company, while EY connects AI to a wider range of business consulting and transformation services.

A possible explanation is that ML6's specialist AI positioning matched the query directly. However, EY also publishes a dedicated AI consulting page, so its absence from my two results cannot be explained simply by a lack of online AI information. Having relevant services online does not guarantee that a company will appear in every AI-generated recommendation.

**What does this result say about the sector?**

AI consulting is a competitive sector where both specialist firms and large consultancies compete for visibility, but AI-generated recommendations can vary even when customers ask the same question.

## Activity 4 — Four-Factor Vulnerability Assessment: ML6

**Company:** ML6  
**Website:** https://www.ml6.eu/  
**Category:** Belgian AI consulting and engineering

ML6 is an AI engineering firm headquartered in Ghent. It sells enterprise AI engineering, AI advisory, AI governance and machine learning solutions.

| **Factor** | **Level** | **Why** |
|---|---|---|
| Visit dependency | **Low** | Revenue comes from large enterprise projects with clients like P&G and Pfizer. No ads, affiliates or subscriptions depend on page visits. |
| AI substitutability | **Low–Medium** | An AI can summarize what ML6 does and its blog benchmarks, but it cannot replace ML6 building and running enterprise AI systems. |
| Intermediary position | **Low** | ML6 is the direct provider, not a middleman. |
| SEO asset weight | **Medium** | It runs a content engine (blog, news), but enterprise relationships and partner channels (OpenAI, AWS, Azure) are the main channel. |

**Matrix position:** Low visit dependency and low-to-medium AI substitutability place ML6 in the **low-risk** quadrant. Its revenue is not directly dependent on website traffic. The real exposure is at the top of the funnel. Buyers shortlisting an AI partner now ask an assistant first, and a firm that is not named may not get onto the shortlist.

**If traffic dropped 60%:** Revenue would barely move in the short term, because deals come through relationships, partner referrals and events. The channel of new, unknown buyers would decrease. ML6 would need to be present where AI models read: partner directories, review sites, press, and question-style content.

## Six-Criterion GEO Audit: ML6

| **Criterion** | **Score** | **Evidence** |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Structured data | **1/3** | The page source has a single JSON-LD block, typed `SiteNavigationElement`, which only maps the menu. There is no `Organization`, `Service`, FAQ or `Review` markup. Meta/OG tags, canonical URL and EN/DE are present. |
| 2. Content parseability | **2/3** | Navigation and page hierarchy are clear. Heading levels are inconsistent, descriptive copy sits in h5 tags, and there is no FAQ or comparison table. |
| 3. Third-party mentions | **2/3** | Google page 1 for “ML6” shows LinkedIn (25.1K followers), OpenAI’s partner page, VAIA (Flanders AI Academy), VTK Gent (a UGent student association), Facebook and a Google Business Profile. Earlier searches added G2, Sortlist, Dealroom and a Tech Scale-up of the Year 2023 award. Most are profiles and directories, and I saw no independent editorial coverage. |
| 4. AI citation presence | **2/3** | I asked ChatGPT, “What are the best AI consulting providers for businesses?” It listed ML6 at #6 of 7 as a “strong Belgian specialist” and placed it in two shortlist rows: custom AI software, and manufacturing and industrial AI. It did not name ML6 first, behind Accenture, McKinsey QuantumBlack, IBM, BCG X and Deloitte. |
| 5. Authority signals | **3/3** | Leadership is named. Cases include Mediahuis and Scout24. The blog publishes original benchmarks (15,728 emails tested) and a field report on LeRobot. Credentials include OpenAI Services Partner, AWS Advanced Tier and Azure partner status. |
| 6. Brand direct-search | **2/3** | Google shows ml6.eu as #1 with sitelinks, a business panel (4.8/5 from 28 reviews), Facebook, YouTube and X profiles, and “People also ask”. There is no Wikipedia page, no news results and no full entity knowledge panel. |
| **Total** | **12/18** | **AI-Present** |

### GEO Audit Conclusion

ML6 is **AI-Present (12/18)**: an AI will often name it, but not necessarily first. Its biggest gaps are technical. The site's structured data covers only navigation, so machines have limited structured confirmation of what ML6 is, and heading levels are inconsistent. The company also has few independent editorial sources, which may help explain why ChatGPT ranked it sixth, behind global consultancies with more third-party coverage.

A generic AI-consulting query still returned ML6 as a Belgian specialist, so it can appear in a recommendation, but as one of several options. The most important technical fix to test is adding `Organization` and `Service` JSON-LD (name, founding date, Ghent address, services, and `sameAs` links to LinkedIn, GitHub and Hugging Face). This would improve the structured-data criterion that ML6 can directly control.
