### My Build — AI Market-Entry Risk Detector

- **Which number proves it?** It correctly classified 18 out of 18 test cases across two test runs, although this does not prove real-world accuracy.

## My click ## 
In Week 2 I believed that if my Risk Detector had weak spots, my test cases would show them as wrong answers. It got all 18 right, including 3 hard ones. But in case C01 (a 20% tax rise plus a currency fall), the tool only flagged the tax risk and ignored the currency, because my prompt tells it to pick just one category. That answer still counted as correct. 
In Week 4 I also realised that 18 cases I wrote myself can't prove my tool never fails. After 18 clean results, the real failure rate could still be up to 16.7% (the Rule of Three). Now I think a perfect score only means my tool works on my own test cases. A correct-looking answer can still miss a risk.
