## 2. Applying the FOMO Framework

| **FOMO question** | **My answer** |
|---|---|
| **1. What specific problem are we trying to solve?** | PwC talks about improving productivity, decision-making and identifying AI opportunities. Before hiring them, a business should identify its actual problem. What problems is it actually facing? Is it high operating costs or slow processes? |
| **2. What happens if we do nothing?** | The company should consider whether its current processes are already working well enough. If there is no major problem, hiring an AI consultant might not be necessary. If competitors are improving their productivity, however, doing nothing could lead to missing opportunities. |
| **3. Can the vendor's claims be verified?** | PwC mentions that 54% of executives say AI has increased productivity in their businesses. I would want to check the original survey and its methodology. I would also ask PwC for examples of completed projects with measurable results. |
| **4. What is the total cost?** | The landing page does not give a specific price for an AI consulting project. A business should ask about consulting fees, software costs, implementation and ongoing maintenance before deciding. |
| **5. Is there a simpler alternative?** | Yes, depending on the problem. A business could first improve its existing processes, use its current data tools or automate simple tasks without hiring a large consulting firm. For complex and bigger AI projects, professional guidance could be worthwhile. |

## 3. My Verdict: Pilot First

I would not hire PwC for a large AI transformation based only on its landing page. PwC describes several possible benefits of AI consulting, but I would request a specific proposal, measurable targets, client examples and a full cost estimate first. A small pilot would help determine whether the service delivers enough value before making a larger investment.

The main lesson is that businesses should not buy AI consulting simply because they fear falling behind competitors. They should identify a real business problem and check whether the proposed solution is worth the cost.
