# Kirill Kyryllov

- **Name:** Kirill Kyryllov
- **Track:** 4 ECTS
- **Beat:** The Fake Analyst: When AI-Generated Business Reports Get It Wrong
- **Why this beat:** I keep asking myself how close AI actually is to replacing human analysts,
  not the marketing version, the real one. Recently I came across a report where AI clearly
  couldn't do the job, and it made me want to dig into this properly: where does AI-generated
  business analysis actually break, and what does that tell us about what still needs a human.
