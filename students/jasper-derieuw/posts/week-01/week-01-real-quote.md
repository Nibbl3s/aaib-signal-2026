## What This Tells Me About Pricing Above the Self Serve Tier

Both ComplyAdvantage and Refinitiv World Check were contacted with identical scenarios and neither responded within the window. This itself is a finding rather than an absence of one. Published, self serve pricing exists for consumer grade AI tools, but at the point where a real compliance use case is described, with actual volume and a specific screening scenario, even the more API first, mid market vendor (ComplyAdvantage) did not engage. Silence from both is consistent with an enterprise, sales led pricing model in this sector: quotes appear to be produced only after a qualified sales conversation, not generated from an inbound email describing scope and volume. This mirrors the argument in my Week 1 Signal post itself: the number that actually decides a compliance AI investment rarely sits on any page a buyer can read without first going through a gatekeeper.

## What This Changes

This finding changes two things in my Week 1 cost model. First, the Cost Modeling Lab treated the three vendor tiers (Budget, Standard, Premium) as if their quoted prices were the whole picture, available to any buyer who asked. The silence from both ComplyAdvantage and Refinitiv World Check shows that a real SME buyer describing a real volume and a real scenario cannot actually obtain a self serve number at all in this market segment, which means the tier prices used in the lab should be treated as illustrative benchmarks rather than numbers a Belgian importer could walk up and pay today. Second, it changes my tier recommendation's realism. My Step 6 recommendation favoured Premium on cost per correctly handled email, but if Premium level vendors in this space are sales gated rather than self serve, the real cost includes a procurement and negotiation cycle that the token and error cost model does not capture at all. A future version of this cost model should add a fourth line, time and effort to even obtain a quote, alongside token cost and error cost, since in this vendor category that line is not zero.
