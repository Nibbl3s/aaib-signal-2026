# What a Company in My Beat Would Have to Change, and Whether It Knows

**The company:** Gaston Schul, a Belgian customs broker that offers sanctions screening as a managed service. My [GEO audit](https://github.com/Nibbl3s/aaib-signal-2026/blob/main/students/jasper-derieuw/evidence/week-05/geo-audit.md) scored it 9 of 18 (AI-Marginal), and it was named in 0 of 6 AI answers to the question a Belgian SME actually asks.

**What it would have to change:**

1. **Be named where the SME asks.** It appears only when a buyer already asks for "a customs broker" (named first in 2 of 3 such questions), never when the buyer asks who can screen Chinese suppliers. The gap is the buyer's wording, not the company's quality.
2. **Be written about by others.** Its own site is well prepared (FAQ markup, an llms.txt, a page that answers "can you run our screening for us?"), but I found only two outside mentions. AI assistants name the companies the rest of the web associates with "sanctions screening", so the work is off-site: trade-support guides, trade press, a client case study.
3. **One page aimed at that buyer:** sanctions screening for Belgian importers, with a price range and plain-language questions and answers.

**Whether it knows:** partly. The llms.txt and FAQ markup show it knows AI systems read its site. It did not answer my direct question in 45 days (one reminder sent on 28 November 2026), so I cannot tell whether it tracks how assistants recommend it, and its preparation targets its own pages, while the gap is outside them.

**What it changed for my work:** this is where the discovery tactics in my capstone memo come from: one identical description across directories, and compliance proof published as citable documents.
