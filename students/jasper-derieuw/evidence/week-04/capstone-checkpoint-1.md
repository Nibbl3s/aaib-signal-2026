# Capstone Checkpoint 1: scenario lock

1. **Company:** a ~150-employee Antwerp-based importer of Chinese electronic components, ~150 shipments and ~1,500 counterparty checks a month (realistic composite).
2. **Decision:** Should they replace fully manual sanctions screening of their China suppliers with rule-based screening where AI only pre-fills invoices and adds warnings, and every risky invoice type still goes to a human?
3. **Reader:** Head of Compliance, with the CFO signing off the budget.
4. **Why it matters:** one missed sanctioned counterparty can mean a fine, legal costs and a possible criminal referral (€50k–€500k in recent Belgian cases, up to €40M under EU law), while manual screening already costs ~€81k/yr in analyst time.
5. **Still to find out:** real time per manual check, the real share of risky invoice types, whether they already pay for a screening tool, and the full cost of a breach beyond the fine.

Traffic light: pending.
