## How to read the silences

Nine messages to five organisations produced no answer at all. In this market, prices, accuracy claims and contract terms are not available to a small buyer who asks in writing, and a federation did not answer a classification question in a month. That is a finding about the market, and it shaped the memo: the recommendation is a pilot with written conditions, not a purchase on published claims.
