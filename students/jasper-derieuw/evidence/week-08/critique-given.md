**The claim I would challenge:** that the AI saves time on the regular checking of legal emails. How much time would actually be saved? The AI cannot make real legal decisions; following the guidelines, it can only suggest, and a person still has to check every suggestion. It is possible, especially as the technology evolves, but the saving was not shown.

**What would change my view:** real numbers and a test on real emails. There was obviously too little time for that during the class.

**My condition for a full yes:** results from the test phase: how much time is saved; how much cost is saved, and after how long; whether it is worth it at all; and whether that changes with a different AI model.
