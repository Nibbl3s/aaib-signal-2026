# Viktor De Ganck

- **Name:** Viktor De Ganck
- **Track:** 4 ECTS
- **Beat:** AI as a retail investor's research desk: can AI turn scattered financial news into a reliable, structured overview?
- **Why this beat:** I have followed financial markets for years, and the information that moves them, from central bank decisions to politics and company news, is spread across sources like the Financial Times, The Economist and X, which makes a structured overview difficult to build. Professional terminals that consolidate this are priced for institutions, so I want to find out whether AI can do it reliably for retail investors, and where it cannot be trusted to.
