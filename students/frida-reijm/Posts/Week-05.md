# Signal Post 5 — Can AI Find LOAVIES? A GEO Audit of a Dutch Fashion Brand

When people look for fashion inspiration, they increasingly ask AI tools for recommendations instead of starting with a traditional search engine. This raises a new question for online retailers: can AI systems actually find and recommend their brand?

## The Company: LOAVIES

For this GEO audit, I chose LOAVIES, a Dutch online fashion retailer founded in 2012 and based in Zwolle. The brand focuses on trendy fashion for young, fashion-minded consumers and offers items such as dresses, tops, jeans, trousers and jackets. LOAVIES regularly introduces new collections and ships internationally. Its online store is central to product discovery and sales.  
Sources: [LOAVIES website](https://www.loavies.com/) · [LOAVIES on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/loavies/)

## The Audit: How Visible Is LOAVIES to AI?

I assessed LOAVIES on six GEO criteria, using a 0–3 scale. The scores below are a preliminary assessment based on publicly visible information; technical checks and repeated AI-prompt tests are still needed to confirm some criteria.

| GEO criterion | Score | Evidence and reasoning |
|---|---:|---|
| Structured Data & Schema Markup | 1/3 | Product pages display product names, prices and categories, but the presence and completeness of structured schema data have not yet been verified in the website source code. |
| Content Parseability | 2/3 | The website has clear product categories and visible product information. This gives search and AI systems useful content to interpret, although the quality of machine-readable product information still needs checking. |
| Third-Party Mention Density | 1/3 | LOAVIES has a LinkedIn company profile and a substantial Trustpilot presence. These provide external references, but the evidence gathered so far does not establish a broad range of independent authoritative mentions. |
| AI Citation Presence | 1/3 | LOAVIES’ appearance in AI-generated fashion recommendations has not yet been systematically tested. This score is therefore provisional rather than proof of consistent AI visibility. |
| Authority & Expertise Signals | 2/3 | The company has operated since 2012 and has an established team and international customer reach. However, the evidence gathered shows limited independent expert content or original research. |
| Brand Direct-Search Strength | 2/3 | LOAVIES has an official website and recognizable profiles on platforms such as LinkedIn and Trustpilot. The audit has not established stronger knowledge-graph signals such as a Wikipedia page or a confirmed knowledge panel. |

**Preliminary total: 9/18 — AI-Marginal.**

## The Verdict: How Vulnerable Is LOAVIES?

LOAVIES has a strong direct retail position, but its online store depends on customers discovering products and visiting the website. That creates high visit dependency. AI can increasingly help shoppers compare styles, find alternatives and receive product recommendations, so some discovery activity could happen without a visit to LOAVIES. However, customers still need the retailer for product availability, sizing, checkout and the actual purchase.

Its intermediary position is relatively low because LOAVIES sells directly to consumers. Its SEO assets remain important, although social media, direct traffic and customer relationships can also support discovery. Overall, I would classify LOAVIES as facing a **moderate vulnerability**: AI may reduce some discovery traffic, but it cannot replace the full shopping experience.

If website traffic dropped by 60%, LOAVIES could lose a significant source of product discovery and potential sales. The impact on revenue would depend on conversion rates and how much traffic comes from channels affected by AI. The brand should strengthen direct customer relationships, improve structured product data and build more independent online authority.

## The Insight: Visibility Needs to Be Earned

The audit suggests that having a recognizable brand and a functioning webshop is not enough. LOAVIES should make its product information easy for AI systems to understand and ensure that reliable external sources mention the brand. It should also regularly test whether AI tools recommend its products for relevant fashion searches. GEO is not only about ranking in search results; it is about being included when customers ask AI what to buy.

**Question:** If shoppers increasingly ask AI where to find their next outfit, how can fashion brands make sure they are part of the answer?
