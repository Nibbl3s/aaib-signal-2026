** Signal Post 3: Does AI Really Increase Fashion Sales? The Omoda and Google Cloud Case** 
The Claim
Google Cloud partnered with Dutch fashion retailer Omoda to improve its online shopping experience using generative AI. Together with implementation partner Rappit, Omoda introduced two features: Omoda Stylist and Discover the Look.
Instead of searching for individual clothing items, customers can describe an outfit they are looking for in natural language. For example, they can search for a bohemian wedding outfit or smart casual workwear. The AI then suggests complete outfits based on preferences, style and favourite brands.
The solution uses Google Cloud technology, including Gemini models, Vertex AI, multimodal embeddings and Vector Search. According to Google Cloud, customers using Discover the Look have a conversion rate 2.5 times higher than customers who do not use the feature.
The Deconstruction
At first sight, this sounds like a strong example of AI improving fashion e-commerce. However, it is important to separate what is actually being offered from what is being promised.
The actual product is an AI-powered shopping assistant that helps customers discover complete outfits. It addresses a recognisable problem in online fashion retail: customers sometimes struggle to combine individual products or find inspiration. By allowing customers to describe what they want, Omoda makes product discovery more conversational and personalised.
The technology is also more than just a chatbot. Google Cloud provides the AI infrastructure and models, while Rappit helped build the data infrastructure and implement the solution. This shows that successful AI implementation also depends on structured product data, integration and technical expertise.
The most interesting claim is the reported 2.5 times higher conversion rate. This sounds impressive, but it does not automatically prove that AI causes customers to buy more. Customers who choose to use the AI feature may already have a stronger intention to purchase. They might be more engaged or spend more time exploring products than other visitors.
To prove that the AI itself increases conversion, Omoda would need to explain how the results were measured. For example, was there a controlled experiment in which similar customers were randomly divided into groups? The public case does not provide enough detail about the sample size, testing period or methodology.
It is also important to distinguish existing features from future ambitions. Avatar-based outfit visualisation and wardrobe integration are mentioned as future developments, not as features already demonstrated in the case.
The FOMO Test
1. What specific business problem will this solve?
The AI helps customers find inspiration and discover complete outfits more easily. This could improve the online shopping experience and product discovery.
2. Can we verify the vendor's claims independently?
The 2.5 times higher conversion rate is reported by Google Cloud and Omoda. However, the public information does not provide enough methodological detail to independently confirm that AI caused the improvement.
3. Is there a simpler, cheaper solution that solves 80% of the problem?
Traditional recommendation systems, curated outfits and improved product filters could also help customers discover matching clothing. Omoda should compare these alternatives with the AI solution to understand its additional value.
The Verdict
PILOT -with clear performance measurements.
The Omoda case demonstrates a relevant application of generative AI in fashion e-commerce. The AI Stylist offers a useful way to make online shopping more personal and interactive. However, the reported conversion improvement needs more transparent evidence before it can be treated as proof of AI's business impact.
My recommendation would be to test the feature against traditional recommendation methods and measure conversion, customer satisfaction and implementation costs. AI should be adopted because it creates measurable value, not simply because it is an exciting technology.
