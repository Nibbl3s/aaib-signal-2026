# AI in Fashion E-commerce: Helpful Tool or Too Much Guesswork?

# The Problem

In fashion e-commerce, writing product descriptions can take a lot of time. Every product needs clear and attractive information about things such as the design, material and fit. AI could help marketers create these descriptions faster.

But can AI always be trusted to write the right information? A product description can sound professional and convincing while still containing information that was never provided. This made me question whether AI is actually the best tool for this task.

# Applying the No-AI Decision Framework

**Q1 – Do we already know the answer?**

For product descriptions, the answer is partly yes. The product information is already available, so AI does not need to create new facts. Its role would mainly be to turn the existing information into a clear and attractive description.

**Q2 – Is the cost of being wrong higher than the cost of being slow?**

Yes, to some extent. If AI adds incorrect information about a product, customers could receive misleading information. For example, AI could mention a material, colour or feature that was never provided. Taking a little more time to check the description is better than publishing incorrect information.

**Q3 – Is the information stable or changing rapidly?**

The basic product information is relatively stable. However, details such as prices, stock and promotions can change quickly. This means AI should not be responsible for creating or guessing this type of information.

**Q4 – Can we verify the AI's answer?**

Yes. This is one of the reasons why this use case could be suitable for AI. A marketer can compare the generated description with the original product information before publishing it. If AI adds something that is not in the input, it can be removed.

**Q5 – What is the simplest solution?**

For this task, AI could be a useful supporting tool. A marketer could provide the product information and let AI create a first draft. The human then checks whether everything is correct before publishing it.

# My AI Build

To investigate this, I am developing a Fashion Product Description Assistant. The tool takes product information as input and creates a short product description.

An important rule in my prompt is that the AI can only use information provided in the input. It should not invent materials, colours, sizes, prices or product features.

I also want to test different types of product information, including short, incomplete and less straightforward inputs. By running the same inputs more than once, I can see whether the AI gives consistent answers and whether it makes the same mistakes.

# My Verdict: AI Can Assist, But Should Not Invent

AI could be useful for fashion e-commerce because it can turn existing product information into a clear first draft quickly. This could save marketers time, especially when many products need descriptions.

However, the fact that an AI-generated description sounds professional does not mean that it is correct. The biggest risk is that AI fills in missing information or makes assumptions.

My conclusion is that AI can be a useful tool for creating product descriptions, but only when the human remains responsible for checking the final result. The goal is not to replace the marketer, but to use AI where it actually adds value.
