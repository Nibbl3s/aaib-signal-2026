Signal Post Week 1 | Cost Literacy | AI in Fashion Marketing & E-commerce

Hook - Why should anyone care about AI costs in your beat?

AI is becoming more common in fashion marketing and e-commerce. Fashion brands can use AI for different tasks, such as writing product descriptions, creating social media content, giving personalized recommendations and helping customers through chatbots. These tools can save a lot of time, but using AI is not completely free. Every time an AI model processes or creates text, it uses tokens. When AI is used thousands of times, these tokens can become an important cost for a company.

The Numbers - What would AI cost for a fashion webshop?

For this example, I looked at a hypothetical fashion webshop that adds 5,000 new products to its website every month. Instead of employees writing every product description themselves, the company uses Claude Sonnet 5.5 to create them.

I estimate that each product description uses around 150 input tokens. This could include information such as the product name, material, colour and style. The generated product description would use around 250 output tokens.

This means the webshop would use:

Input:
5,000 × 150 = 750,000 input tokens per month

Output:
5,000 × 250 = 1,250,000 output tokens per month

According to Anthropic, Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs $2 per 1 million input tokens and $10 per 1 million output tokens.

The monthly cost would therefore be:

Input cost:
750,000 / 1,000,000 × $2 = $1.50

Output cost:
1,250,000 / 1,000,000 × $10 = $12.50

Total monthly AI cost:
$1.50 + $12.50 = $14.00

Using the exchange rate from the course of $1 = €0.92:

$14.00 × €0.92 = €12.88 per month

So, generating 5,000 product descriptions would cost around €12.88 per month in token costs.

The Insight - What surprised me?

What surprised me most is how low the token cost actually is. Creating 5,000 product descriptions sounds like a huge task, but the AI token cost would only be around €13 per month.

However, this does not mean that the total cost of using AI is only €13. A fashion company would still need people to check the descriptions, make sure the information is correct and keep the content consistent with the brand. There could also be costs for software and integrating the AI into the webshop. This shows that token cost is only one part of the total cost of using AI.

The Question - What do I want to learn next?

I want to learn more about how fashion webshops use AI for personalization. For example, can AI recommendations actually make customers buy more products? I would like to find out whether the extra sales from personalization are worth the costs of implementing and using AI.
