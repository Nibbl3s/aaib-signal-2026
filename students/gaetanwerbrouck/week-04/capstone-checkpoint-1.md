# Capstone Checkpoint 1 — Scenario Lock

1. **The company:** Flanders United FC (a realistic composite Belgian professional club with a first-team recruitment department, three scouts and an estimated 200 player profiles reviewed each month).
2. **The decision:** Should Flanders United fund an AI-assisted player-screening tool for structured player statistics, or use a spreadsheet/database filter instead?
3. **The reader:** Head of Recruitment, who controls scouting workflow and tool budget.
4. **Why it matters:** If the club chooses poorly, it could spend setup and support budget on a tool that adds complexity without improving the shortlist, or miss a promising player because the screening process uses stale or incorrect data.
5. **What I still need to find out:** The real monthly profile volume and manual review time, the actual loaded cost of scouting/analysis staff, the data licence and integration costs, and whether a spreadsheet already handles the structured screening reliably.

**Decision test:** This is answerable as yes/no: should the club fund the AI tool rather than use a spreadsheet/database filter? Current provisional answer: no for fixed five-rule screening; investigate AI only for unstructured reports where it may add incremental value.
