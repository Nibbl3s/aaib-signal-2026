# Can AI Find an AI Football Scout? A GEO Audit of Oraca

My beat is how football clubs use data to find undervalued players. On 21 October 2026, I ran the same ChatGPT recommendation prompt twice, asking for three AI-powered football scouting or player-discovery platforms. The first answer named SciSports, TransferLab and Hudl Wyscout; the second named SciSports, TransferRoom Scout and SkillCorner. SciSports appeared both times, but the other recommendations changed. The answers were partly repeatable, not identical.

Oraca was the relevant omission I investigated. Its website calls it “AI player discovery for recruitment teams” and says it is in “Private beta.” The homepage reports 327 players flagged in a blind 2018–2023 backtest, a 4.8× average return, and 12% of picks returning 10× or more. These are Oraca’s own figures, not independently verified results. Its research page says first public scoring of a pre-registered forward test is planned for 30 June 2027.

## GEO scorecard (provisional)

- **Structured data/schema: 2/3 — provisional.** A supplied page-source excerpt showed JSON-LD using schema.org and an Organization item. Google’s Rich Results Test detected “one valid item” under “Software apps”; fetch succeeded and crawling/indexing were allowed. It also reported non-critical issues, whose details were not captured. I score this 2/3 because one valid item is confirmed, but the evidence does not establish every rich schema type described in the rubric.
- **Content parseability: 3/3.** The homepage uses clear workflow headings, “Surface,” “Scout,” and “Reach,” and a “Questions, answered” FAQ section.
- **Third-party mentions: 0/3.** My Google-focused search for “Oraca football scouting” returned zero qualifying independent results across the first two result pages I reviewed. This is limited to that query and date, not proof that no independent mention exists anywhere.
- **AI recommendation presence: 1/3.** SciSports appeared in both tested answers; Oraca appeared in neither. This is only a two-run result for one prompt.
- **Authority and expertise: 2/3.** Oraca says its signals are “validated out-of-sample” and describes a pre-registered forward test. These are useful transparency signals, but they are company-published and the forward test is not yet publicly scored.
- **Direct brand-search strength: 2/3 — preliminary.** For “oraca,” Google’s AI Overview described the football platform as “An AI-powered player discovery and scouting tool” using data across “60+ leagues,” and the organic results included `oraca.ai`. But the overview also listed unrelated entities with similar names, and no dedicated knowledge panel was visible in my captured results.

The updated total is **10/18, AI-Marginal** under the course bands (7–11). I scored structured data 2/3 because one valid item is confirmed, but the evidence does not establish the full range of rich schema types described in the rubric. The exact issue details and some technical checks remain unverified.

Compared with Oraca, SciSports has a broader visible set of scouting and valuation pages and has announced an RBFA collaboration. Oraca’s positioning and methodology are explicit, but independent corroboration is limited. A 60% fall in search visits could reduce awareness and enquiries if search is important; it would not automatically mean a 60% revenue decline.

My vulnerability ratings are visit dependency **Medium**, AI substitutability **Low**, intermediary position **Low**, and SEO asset weight **Medium**. Overall vulnerability looks moderate, with low confidence. Site-wide indexability and the detailed schema warnings remain unverified.

**Question:** How much should a GEO audit reward a clear methodology page when independent corroboration and AI recommendations remain limited?
