# Recorded Pitch 1: script (3 minutes)

About 345 spoken words, roughly 2:30 to 2:50. Rehearse out loud against the clock, then stop reading it and talk. The recommendation comes in the first 15 seconds.

**Recommendation and ask (0:00)**

"My recommendation: Flanders United's Head of Recruitment should not fund an AI screening tool. Keep the screening in a filter the club controls, like my tool ScoutLab, and use AI only as an optional layer for written notes that can be swapped out."

**Company and decision**

"The company is Flanders United, a club where three scouts review about 200 player profiles a month. The decision is whether to fund an AI-assisted screening tool, or use a spreadsheet or database filter instead."

**The number**

"The number this turns on is 4 out of 20. I had a small AI model, running free on my own laptop, write scouting notes for ten players, twice. I checked all 20 notes automatically against the data. A note only counted if it named the right strongest and weakest stat and invented nothing. The free model got 4 right. Claude got 19.

That's my own measurement on a small sample, so I'm confident about the direction, not the exact gap.

Here's why it matters. The free model isn't good enough, so there is a case for paying for a model. But it's a cheap case: about a cent per run, and switching takes about an hour. So the money isn't in the model, and neither is the lock-in risk."

**Lock-in**

"The lock-in risk is the data. My scorecard puts the stack at 5 out of 15, moderate. My app's default data comes from one GitHub file I don't own, with no licence and no promise anyone warns me if it moves. Anthropic gives 60 days' notice before retiring a model, but that's in the documentation, not the contract."

**Weakness and what would change my mind**

"My weakest assumption is volume. I estimated 200 profiles a month, and I haven't measured it. If a real club screens ten times that, the cost changes, and so might my answer. I'd also change my mind if scouts won't use a written summary when they can already read the chart, or if a licensed data feed has bad export terms."

**Close**

"So: keep screening in a filter you control, keep any AI swappable, and ask for export terms before you sign anything."
