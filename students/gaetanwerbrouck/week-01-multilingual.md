\## Questions



\### 1. Which document increased the most in tokens when translated to Dutch?



Document 3 had the largest increase in raw tokens, increasing by 71 tokens from 157 to 228. Document 2 had the highest percentage increase, at 45.9%.



\### 2. For the most expensive document, what is the annual cost difference if processed 1,000 times per year?



Document 3 costs $4.71 per year in English and $6.84 per year in Dutch when processed 1,000 times. The difference is $2.13 per year.



\### 3. Should a customer service chatbot force customers to use English to reduce costs?



Using English can reduce token usage, but forcing customers to use English could make the chatbot less accessible and less natural for Dutch-speaking customers. The additional token cost of Dutch is small for short texts, so the business would need to consider both cost and customer experience.
