# Can AI Find Dignify? A Marketing Agency GEO Audit

## The Hook

What happens when a customer asks an AI assistant for three marketing agencies instead of clicking through Google results? The answer becomes a shortlist, and companies not mentioned may lose a chance to be considered. For agencies selling personalized marketing services, this is a new visibility risk.

## The Company

I audited [Dignify](https://www.dignify.agency/), a Belgian marketing-technology agency based in Genk. It offers CRM, marketing automation and customer-data services. Its website includes client work for JBC and Bol. I selected it because its services are relevant to my beat, AI in Marketing and Consumer Personalization, yet I wanted to test whether AI actually recommends it.

## The Six-Criterion GEO Audit

I used the course's 0–3 scoring system. My **sampled result is 8/18: AI-Marginal**, with limitations noted below.

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Structured Data | **0/3** | On 23 October, I checked the homepage and [marketing-automation page](https://www.dignify.agency/service/marketing-automation/) source with `Ctrl+U`. Searches for `application/ld+json` and `schema.org` found no matches on those pages; I cannot rule out markup elsewhere or added dynamically. |
| Content Parseability | **2/3** | The [services page](https://www.dignify.agency/services/) uses headings such as “Core Services” and “Marketing Automation & CDP”, plus platform lists. It is understandable, though I did not see a comprehensive Q&A or comparison structure in the reviewed content. |
| Third-Party Mentions | **2/3** | [IKAg](https://www.ikag.be/en/factsheets/dignify) calls Dignify a “Data, CRM & martech agency”. [Salesforce AppExchange](https://appexchange.salesforce.com/appxConsultingListingDetail?listingId=00f093e9-253a-478b-812c-b91878204867), LinkedIn and other external directories also list it. I did not exhaustively count Google results. |
| AI Citation Presence | **0/3** | With the same e-commerce CRM recommendation prompt, Gemini gave **Scalify, Fourfront, Springbok** on run one, then **Saluton, Chipper, Customer Impact** on run two. ChatGPT gave **Pebble, The Dot Society, Fourfront**. Dignify appeared **zero times in three responses**. |
| Authority Signals | **2/3** | Dignify identifies its managing director and publishes named [client cases](https://www.dignify.agency/cases/). Its Salesforce profile also provides external expertise information. |
| Brand Direct-Search | **2/3 provisional** | Searches found Dignify's website, LinkedIn and external agency profiles; I did not verify its exact-name position or a Google knowledge panel. |

## The Verdict

The most surprising result was not only Dignify's absence. The **two Gemini runs shared zero recommended companies**, despite my using the same question. This means a single AI result should not be treated as a permanent ranking. Dignify has real services, public case studies and independent mentions, but it was absent from my specific sampled shortlist. Its 8/18 score is therefore a limited GEO snapshot, not proof that the company is unsuccessful or always invisible to AI.

My practical recommendation is to check whether structured service and organization data can be added, publish easily verifiable details about CRM specializations and client outcomes, and repeat recommendation tests across different prompts over time.

## The Market Insight

Dignify is less vulnerable than a recipe blog whose revenue depends directly on clicks. AI can summarize what an agency offers, but cannot replace complex CRM implementation. However, it can still influence which providers buyers contact first. For marketing agencies, being a credible option is different from being included in an AI-generated shortlist.

## The Question

How should an agency measure whether AI recommendations bring qualified leads, rather than merely tracking mentions?
