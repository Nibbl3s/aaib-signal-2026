**Why AI CV Screening Doesn’t Break on Token Prices, But on Recruiters Who Blindly Trust AI**
The HookHR departments are embracing AI to process job applications faster. On paper, API costs seem negligible. But anyone who only looks at the pricing page misses the largest cost component. I modeled the API and error costs for CV screening — and the break point isn't where you'd expect.The Use CaseAn HR organization deploys AI to analyze job postings and match incoming CVs. For each screening, the AI reads the job requirements (500 tokens), the CV (800 tokens), and generates a structured evaluation (400 tokens). A recruiter processes an average of 50 CVs per day.The NumbersBased on a measurement using gemini-3.1-flash-lite and projected onto Standard Tier ($2.00/1M input, $12.00/1M output), a single CV screening costs $0.0074 (€0.0068).   At 10 recruiters (10,000 CVs/month), the token cost is €68.08 per month.At 1,000 recruiters (1,000,000 CVs/month), the token cost increases to €6,808.00 per month.Compared to the human baseline (€7.50 per CV at 10 minutes of manual work), 1 million AI screenings yield a theoretical saving of over €7.4 million per month.The Break PointThe break point in HR does not lie in API volume, but in the error rate. If the AI makes an incorrect evaluation in 15% of cases and a recruiter needs 10 minutes (€5.00) to fix it, the error costs at 100,000 CVs reach €75,000 per month — over 110 times higher than the token bill of €680.80.As long as recruiters only correct errors, the business case remains strong. But the real break point occurs when the error rate increases and recruiters lose trust. As soon as recruiters start double-checking every CV manually as a precaution, you pay for both the AI infrastructure and the labor costs, turning ROI into a loss.The InsightIn HR automation, token pricing is noise on the wire. Anyone designing an AI recruiting system should not negotiate over token discounts, but invest in reducing the error rate (via RAG and better prompts) and creating an efficient human validation step.The QuestionIf the error rate is the single variable that determines the business case: which HR department is currently tracking the False Positive Rate of its AI recruiters on a monthly basis?
