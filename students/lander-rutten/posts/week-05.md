**Can  AI Find a Belgian HR-Tech Startup? A GEO Audit of Joobs**

Hook

When an HR manager asks an AI assistant, "What is the best AI tool for automating CV screening in Belgian SMEs?", which platforms appear in the answer? I ran a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) audit on an emerging local startup to find out—and the results highlight a major blind spot for European HR tech.

The Company

Joobs is a Zellik-based HR-tech startup originating from the Start it @KBC incubator. They offer an AI-powered recruitment platform designed to help SMEs automate candidate matching, parse CVs, and streamline hiring through their AI copilot, SAGE. I selected Joobs because they represent the exact type of innovative, mid-sized regional player that should surface when an AI is asked for local vendor recommendations, but likely does not.

The Audit

I evaluated Joobs across the 6 GEO criteria:

Structured Data (1/3): The site lacks explicit JSON-LD Schema (SoftwareApplication), leaving AI engines guessing about their precise features.

Content Parseability (2/3): Well-structured for human eyes, but lacks structured comparison tables or direct Q&A formats that AI crawlers easily extract.

Third-Party Mentions (2/3): Present in Flemish tech news, but absent on software review aggregators like G2 or Capterra.

AI Citation Presence (0/3): AI assistants never recommend Joobs; they default to established global competitors like Textkernel or Workable.

Authority Signals (1/3): Limited original research or expert-bylined articles on AI hiring ethics.

Brand Direct-Search (1/3): Search queries easily get confused with generic "jobs" queries without a Knowledge Panel.

The Verdict

Joobs scored 7/18 — AI-Marginal. An AI agent asked to recommend a CV screening vendor for Flemish SMEs would fail to mention them. Their product may be tailored and effective, but their digital footprint leaves them virtually invisible to AI-mediated discovery. Their primary bottleneck is AI Citation Presence and low third-party domain authority; rely solely on your own website, and AI models will lack the multi-source corroboration needed to recommend you.

The Insight

This audit highlights a broader shift in the HR-tech landscape: startups are spending heavily on local PR and traditional SEO, but remain completely unoptimized for generative AI engines. Early-moving HR software vendors that implement structured data and build third-party citation networks today will capture an outsized share of prospective clients as AI becomes the primary gatekeeper.

The Question

How quickly will regional B2B software vendors adapt to GEO before traditional search traffic declines beyond recovery?
