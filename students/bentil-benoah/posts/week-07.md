The Week 2 cloud log records 10/10 in both runs. That gives an 80 percentage-point gap under this strict rule. The dates and interfaces differ, which limits the comparison. Many local replies were category numbers. For the double-payment enquiry, the first reply was “1. Order Status” and the second was “2”. Both the format and consistency need work.
