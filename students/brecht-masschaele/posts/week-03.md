## Hook

Our internship & BAP coordination hub already runs part of its admin on Claude. 
A paid plan for the whole hub feels like the logical next step. 
Before anyone signs, I want four answers: what happens to student data? 
What will actions and monitoring cost per year? 
How much time will we save? 
And will every hub user need a premium seat? 🔍

## The Claim

Anthropic sells Claude to universities through Team, Enterprise and Education plans. Three claims carry the pitch:

- Claude "meets the compliance requirements higher education institutions have for student data" (education page).
- Claude helps administrative teams "transform the way they work without losing quality" (education page).
- A Team seat costs $20 a month (billed annually); a Premium seat gives "5x more usage than standard seats" (pricing page).

Add logos from LSE, Northeastern and Dartmouth, and the pitch looks complete.

## The Deconstruction

| Claim | Tag | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "No model training on your content by default" (Team) | **Fact** | Checkable in the plan table and terms. But Free and Pro are opt-out, so a coach on a personal account isn't covered. |
| University logos | **Fact** | Verifiable, but it proves adoption, not results. |
| "Transform the way they work without losing quality" | **Aspiration** | No baseline, no metric, no timeframe. |
| Security "designed to meet institutional compliance needs" | **Aspiration** | "Designed to" describes intent, not outcome. |
| "Meets the compliance requirements … for student data" | **Avoidance** | Which ones? No mention of GDPR, a data processing agreement, or where EU student data is processed. |
| Premium = "5x more usage than standard seats" | **Avoidance** | 5x of what? Standard usage isn't defined, and limits can change at Anthropic's discretion. |
| Enterprise usage cost "scales with model and task" | **Avoidance** | "It depends." What a scheduled monitoring task costs isn't on the page. |

Two facts, two aspirations, three avoidances. And the avoidances sit exactly on my first two questions: data and cost.

## The FOMO Test

**Q1: What specific problem?** "AI for the hub" is FOMO. A real answer names one task with a current metric, like hours per week spent on repeat questions from students and coaches. I don't have that number yet.

**Q3: Can we verify independently?** Yes, and this is Anthropic's strongest point. Team bills monthly from two seats, no sales call needed. No "only two pilot slots left".

**Q4: What's the total cost?** Five standard seats: 5 × $20 × 12 = $1,200, or **€1,104 a year** (est.). Not visible: whether scheduled monitoring pushes us onto Premium seats (five times the price), extra usage at API rates, and our own time maintaining skills. If all 40 coaches need a seat, that's **€8,832 a year** (est.) before anyone saves an hour.

## The Verdict

**Pilot.** This isn't FOMO: the product is real, the training claim is checkable, and monthly billing makes testing cheap. But the questions that matter most for a Belgian university, data and cost at our volume, are exactly the ones the pages avoid.

So: three months on Team, billed monthly, core hub team only (3 seats × $25 × 3 = **€207**). No identifiable student data until our data protection officer has seen the DPA. Measure one named task before and after, and check the usage meter weekly. Buy or walk away in January.

## The Question

If standard seats hold up for the core team, do coaches need seats at all, or can the hub deliver the AI's output to them without an account each? 🎓
