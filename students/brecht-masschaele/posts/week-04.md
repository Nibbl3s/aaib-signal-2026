## Hook

In three weeks I priced an email (week 1), refused AI for a form (week 2) and recommended a pilot (week 3). What I never did was answer the question my director will ask first: what is it worth? In higher education, "AI will save us time" is the default pitch, and almost nobody puts a euro figure on it. So here's mine, for the internship & BAP coordination hub. 📊

## The Candidate

**Drafting replies to repeat questions in the hub's shared inbox.** Students, coaches and company mentors ask the same things every semester: deadlines, which form goes where, insurance, how the BAP rubric works.

In a programme's value chain, internships and the BAP are a **primary activity**: work-integrated learning is part of what the student comes for. The inbox is the support layer that keeps that activity running.

Through the 5-criterion filter: repetitive (every week), pattern-based (question → known answer), error-tolerant (a staff member reviews every draft before it's sent), cost-justifiable (to be tested below), verifiable (the reviewer either sends, edits or rewrites). Verdict: **candidate, with guardrails.**

## The Numbers

**Assumptions** (all est., to be replaced with pilot data):

- 600 emails/month × 10 active months = **6,000 emails/year**; 40% are repeat questions = **2,400**
- Today: 8 min per reply. With AI: 2 min to review a good draft
- Loaded rate for hub staff: €48,000 × 1.4 ÷ 1,600 h = **€42/hr**
- Draft success rate: **80%** on my test set. A failed draft costs 10 min (2 min review, then 8 min writing it myself): **2 min worse** than today

**Benefits**

| Category | Annual value | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Time saved (at 100%) | €10,080 | 2,400 × 6 min = 240 h × €42 |
| **Time saved (at 80%)** | **€7,392** | (1,920 × 6 min − 480 × 2 min) = 176 h × €42 |
| Revenue increase | €0 | Faster replies don't add students or fees |
| Cost reduction | €0 | No licence or tool is replaced |
| Risk mitigation | ≈ €0 | Fewer contradictory answers between staff, but offset by a new risk: a fabricated deadline (F2) |
| **Gross benefit** | **€7,392** | |

**Costs**

| Item | Year 1 | Year 2+ |
|---|---:|---:|
| 3 Team seats (3 × $25 × 12 × 0.92) | €828 | €828 |
| Building and testing the FAQ + skill (40 h × €42) | €1,680 | — |
| FAQ update when rules change (2 × 6 h × €42) | €504 | €504 |
| **Total AI cost** | **€3,012** | **€1,332** |

Tokens aren't a separate line: they're included in the seats. Even on the API, week 1's maths puts them at around €20 a year. Noise.

**Net benefit:** €7,392 − €3,012 = **€4,380 in Year 1**, **€6,060 from Year 2**.
**Payback:** €1,680 one-time ÷ (€6,060 ÷ 12 = €505/month) = **3.3 months**.

One honest caveat: 176 hours is **capacity, not cash**. Nobody's salary goes down. The value only becomes real if those hours go somewhere: in our case, to the logbook follow-up and early-warning work I flagged in week 2.

## The Pressure Test

**What if my benefits are 50% too optimistic?** Gross drops to €3,696. Year 1 nets **€684**, Year 2+ **€2,364**. The case survives, but only just in Year 1.

**What success rate does it need?** Value = 2,400 × (8r − 2) ÷ 60 × €42. Break-even is **47% in Year 1** and **35% from Year 2**. My 80% has room to fall.

**The real weak spot isn't the rate, it's the failure type.** A draft that misses a nuance (F3) costs me two minutes. A draft that invents a deadline (F2) and slips past a tired reviewer costs a student their internship start. The rate can't price that, so the guardrail can't be optional: every reply is reviewed, and every date is pulled from the FAQ, never generated.

## The Insight

The number is small. €6,000 a year is a few weeks of one person's time, not a transformation. And that's exactly the point: the case holds because the cost is tiny, not because the benefit is big. Anyone pitching "AI for student services" at programme scale should be able to show this table for one inbox first.

My takeaway for the beat: **in higher education, AI's ROI is mostly redeemed capacity.** If you can't say where the freed hours go, you don't have a business case. You have a nicer inbox. 💡
