## Activity 2: Class Consolidation & Data Gathering

During this session, we consolidated data across all 40 student beats to evaluate how Generative AI engine queries interact with various industry sectors, company sizes, and digital footprints.

### Key Observations:
1. **Name Recognition Disparity:** AI models consistently output named, real-world entities for high-density B2C consumer sectors (e.g., travel, retail, consumer tech) while frequently returning generic recommendations or silence for niche B2B consulting services.
2. **Third-Party Dependency:** Companies relying exclusively on self-published website claims struggled for visibility compared to entities actively discussed across third-party media, academic journals, and review aggregators.
3. **Dynamic Output Volatility:** Running identical queries across separate threads yielded differing company rankings, confirming that traditional "search engine ranking" (SEO) is replaced by probabilistic synthesis in AI systems.

## Activity 3: Find the Pattern

Analyzing the consolidated dataset of all 40 class beats reveals clear structural patterns regarding where AI names real entities versus where it returns no actionable results.

### 1. Analysis of Tested Theories Against Data
* **B2C vs. B2B:** B2C sectors benefit from dense online consumer reviews and aggregator platforms, allowing AI to extract structured recommendations effortlessly. B2B niche sectors (such as workplace psychology consulting or specialized industrial machinery) frequently face AI silence or generic lists of Tier-1 legacy firms (e.g., McKinsey, Gartner).
* **Presence of Third-Party Sources:** The decisive factor for AI discovery is not the quality of a firm's owned website, but the **density of third-party coverage** (press articles, Reddit discussions, professional directories, academic citations). AI synthesizes what *others* say about an entity.
* **Market Leaders vs. Most Mentioned:** Entities recommended by AI are not necessarily technical market leaders; they are **entities with the highest overall text density and digital footprint**.
* **Result Volatility:** Re-running identical queries yielded shifting recommendations, proving that fixed algorithmic rank (SEO) has dissolved into dynamic probabilistic generation.

### 2. Core Emerging Thesis
AI does not search for the "best" company; it searches for entities that are **easiest to synthesize** and **most heavily corroborated by third-party text sources**. If an industry relies on opaque trust networks, unstructured websites, or offline relationships, AI effectively ignores it.

## Activity 4: GEO Audit Framework & Vulnerability Taxonomies

This activity formalizes the transition from traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

### 1. Comparison: SEO Era (2020) vs. AI Agent Era (2026)

| Dimension | SEO Era (2020) | AI Agent Era (2026) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What the user sees** | 10 ranked links + featured snippet | Single synthesized response with inline citations |
| **Sites visited by user** | Typically 2 to 4 websites | 0 to 1 website (Zero-click resolution) |
| **Who controls visibility** | Google ranking algorithms | LLM synthesis logic (probabilistic & opaque) |
| **Discovery channel** | Organic search & paid ads | AI citations (no paid placement available) |
| **Unit of attention** | A Click (CTR) | A Mention / Citation |
| **Ranks 4 to 10 status** | Still receive organic traffic | Invisible (AI typically recommends 2-3 entities) |
| **Threatened business models** | Ad-funded content sites | Ad networks, affiliate review sites, directories |
| **Key metric to track** | Organic Traffic, CTR, Keyword Rank | AI Citation Frequency, Brand Mention Density |
| **What companies control** | Keywords, backlinks, ad spend | Structured data, third-party press, brand authority |

### 2. Business Model Vulnerability Taxonomy
Four factors determine a firm's exposure to AI disintermediation:
* **Visit Dependency:** Extent to which revenue requires direct website visits (ad views, transactional checkouts).
* **AI Substitutability:** Ability of AI to fully satisfy user intent without requiring the source website.
* **Intermediary Position:** Reliance on acting as a middleman, directory, or affiliate aggregator.
* **SEO Asset Weight:** Vulnerability caused by historical over-reliance on legacy SEO rankings.

### 3. The 6-Criterion GEO Audit Framework
1. **Structured Data & Schema Markup (0-3):** Implementation of JSON-LD / Schema.org machine-readable metadata.
2. **Content Parseability (0-3):** Formatting clarity (H1/H2, bulleted lists, Q&A tables) for AI extraction.
3. **Third-Party Mention Density (0-3):** Frequency and authority of external brand citations.
4. **AI Citation Presence (0-3):** Direct appearance in AI answer engine queries.
5. **Authority & Expertise Signals (0-3):** Named authors, academic credentials, whitepapers, case studies.
6. **Brand Direct-Search Strength (0-3):** Brand recognition, Knowledge Panel presence, direct search volume.
