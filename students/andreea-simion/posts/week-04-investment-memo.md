SUBJECT:  AI Investment Recommendation — Automated Employee Sentiment Classifier

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
We recommend deploying an automated NLP sentiment classifier to triage monthly employee AI feedback, requiring an initial Year 1 expenditure of €2,981. The system delivers €10,710 in annual gross value with a net Year 1 benefit of €7,729 and a payback period of 0.8 months.

THE PROBLEM
The HR department receives approximately 300 unstructured employee feedback comments per month regarding software rollouts and workplace AI adoption. Manual reading, categorizing, and logging requires 5 minutes per comment, consuming 25 hours per month (300 hours annually) of HR recruiter time. At a loaded Belgian market rate of €44/hour, this routine administrative sorting costs the organization €13,200 annually while delaying critical interventions on employee anxiety.

THE PROPOSAL
We propose deploying a lightweight LLM classifier to instantly categorize incoming feedback into six core psychological barrier categories (e.g., Job Security Fear, Trust/Accuracy Concern, Change Fatigue). HR staff will transition from manual sorting to spot-checking flagged logs and edge cases, reducing review time from 5 minutes to 1 minute per comment. Prompt tuning, initial test validation, and script integration will be handled internally with human-in-the-loop audit guardrails maintained for all outputs.

RISKS & MITIGATIONS
In empirical stress testing, the classifier achieved a 70% pass rate, exhibiting F6 (Inconsistent classification) on mixed sentiment and F2 (Fabricated context) on short inputs. To address capability skepticism and prevent ungrounded algorithmic claims, HR analysts will maintain a strict Human-in-the-Loop review protocol, auditing 100% of flagged edge cases and spot-checking routine logs before triggering interventions.

RECOMMENDATION
Proceed with a 60-day pilot deployment for all internal AI adoption feedback channels. Approval is contingent on enforcing mandatory human oversight to absorb the measured 30% error rate without affecting policy decisions.

SENSITIVITY NOTE
If labor time savings are 50% lower than projected (10 hours/month saved instead of 20), annual time-saved value drops to €5,280, yielding a reduced Year 1 net benefit of €2,449. Even under this highly conservative scenario, the business case remains positive with a payback period under 2 months.
