EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
We recommend deploying an automated NLP sentiment classifier to triage monthly employee AI feedback, requiring an initial Year 1 expenditure of €2,981 (including €2,376 in human error correction)[cite: 3]. The system delivers €10,710 in annual gross value with a net Year 1 benefit of €7,729 and a payback period of 0.8 months[cite: 3].

THE PROBLEM
The HR department receives approximately 300 unstructured employee feedback comments per month regarding software rollouts and workplace AI adoption[cite: 3]. Manual reading, categorizing, and logging requires 5 minutes per comment, consuming 25 hours per month (300 hours annually) of HR recruiter time[cite: 3]. At a loaded Belgian market rate of €44/hour, this routine administrative sorting costs the organization €13,200 annually while delaying critical interventions on employee anxiety and change fatigue[cite: 3].

THE PROPOSAL
We propose deploying a lightweight LLM classifier to instantly categorize incoming feedback into six core psychological barrier categories (e.g., Job Security Fear, Trust/Accuracy Concern, Change Fatigue)[cite: 3]. HR staff will transition from manual sorting to spot-checking flagged logs and edge cases, reducing review time from 5 minutes to 1 minute per comment[cite: 3]. Prompt tuning, initial test validation, and script integration will be handled internally with human-in-the-loop audit guardrails maintained for all outputs[cite: 1, 3].

RISKS & MITIGATIONS
In empirical stress testing across 10 representative test cases, the classifier achieved a 70% pass rate, exhibiting F6 (Inconsistent classification) on mixed sentiment and F2 (Fabricated context) on ultra-short inputs[cite: 1, 3]. In an HR setting, hallucinated employee motives create organizational noise if unmonitored[cite: 1]. To mitigate this, HR analysts will maintain a strict Human-in-the-Loop review protocol, auditing 100% of flagged edge cases and spot-checking 20% of routine logs before triggering workplace interventions[cite: 1, 3].

RECOMMENDATION
Proceed with a 60-day pilot deployment for all internal AI adoption feedback channels[cite: 3]. Approval is contingent on enforcing mandatory human oversight to absorb the measured 30% error rate without affecting policy decisions[cite: 1, 3].

SENSITIVITY NOTE

Even under this highly conservative scenario, the business case remains positive with a payback period under 2 months[cite: 3].
