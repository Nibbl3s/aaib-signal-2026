**[0:00 — the ask, ≤15 s]**
**"DJ Thorax, my own one-person DJ business, should *not* put AI on the booking-inquiry-to-quote step. Keep the booking form and a fixed price table, and spend the time on gigs."**

**[0:15 — company and decision]**
I get about 35 serious inquiries a year: 13 through my form and mail in 15 months, which is my own count, plus an estimated two a month on Instagram, WhatsApp and the phone. The decision is whether an AI that reads those inquiries and tells me what's still missing before a quote is worth using.

**[0:35 — the number it turns on]**
**"The number is the error rate on one field: who brings the decks."**
I built the tool and tested it on 15 real inquiries, twice each: 30 outputs. On Claude Haiku, 4 of 30 said "the decks are on site" when I actually had to bring my own. That's 13%. This week I ran the exact same test on a model on my own laptop: same two inquiries, same mistake, again 13%.
That error doesn't cost minutes, it costs a gig. I turn up without decks: about €300 and my reputation.
The tool saves me at most 7½ minutes per inquiry. At €20 an hour and 35 inquiries, that's **€87.50 a year, at 100% accuracy.**
To be worth running unchecked, the decks error has to fall below €2.50 ÷ €300, so **under 1%**. I measured 13%, on two different models.

**[1:40 — what that means]**
The token bill is irrelevant: about a tenth of a cent a month on Gemini. The cost of AI here isn't the vendor's price. It's who pays for the wrong field, and in a one-person business that's always me. With a human check on the decks field it breaks even only in year two, at about €32 a year. That's not worth building.

**[2:05 — what I still need to find out]**
**"My weakest number is the 7½ minutes."** It's my own estimate. I'm timing my next five inquiries. If it's really 20 minutes, the upside triples, but it's still under one decks mistake a year.
Second, the chat and phone volume is an estimate. I'm logging it from now on.
And third, fifteen test inquiries can't prove an error rate under 1%. That would take more than a hundred. **"If a bigger test set showed a fixed prompt never gets the decks wrong, I'd change my mind, for the reading step only, never for gear or price."**

**[2:45 — close]**
So: no AI on inquiry-to-quote for DJ Thorax. The form already asks the right questions, for free.
