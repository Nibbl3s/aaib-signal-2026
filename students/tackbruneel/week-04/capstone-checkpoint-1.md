# Capstone Checkpoint 1 — Scenario lock

1. **The company:** DJ Thorax: my own one-person DJ business in West Flanders (Izegem). About 4 gigs a month (private parties, weddings, youth-movement parties, company events), booked through a Google booking form, e-mail and Instagram/WhatsApp. Sound & light are subcontracted to a partner (VisionSound). Invoices go through Accountable.
2. **The decision:** Should DJ Thorax automate inquiry handling and quoting with AI (an inquiry extractor plus AI-drafted replies), or keep the booking form plus a rule-based price table and answer by hand?
3. **The reader:** The owner-manager of DJ Thorax (me), the only person who can say yes. Second reader: a partner like VisionSound, who would use the same flow for sound & light quotes.
4. **Why it matters:** One wrong quote or one gig where I show up without gear costs about €150–300 plus reputation. My Week 2 Build got only 50% of inquiries fully right and twice said gear was on site when it wasn't. A slow reply, on the other hand, can lose a booking worth €240–700.
5. **What I still need to find out:**
   - My real inquiry volume per channel (I'm logging Instagram/WhatsApp from now on; estimate 1–3/month on top of about 0.9/month via form and mail).
   - How many inquiries I lose today through slow replies (no data yet).
   - Whether a v2 prompt gets the gear and time fields reliably right.
   - What a bad gig really costs me (one lost fee, or a lost client plus referrals).

**Self-check:** 🟡 — the decision is a clear choose-between and the reader can say yes, but the stakes are small in euros. If the teacher thinks the size is too small for a capstone, the fallback is the same decision for VisionSound (more quotes, higher order values).
