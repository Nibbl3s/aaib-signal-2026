## The thread through Weeks 1–4

1. **Week 1:** the token bill for an AI quote bot is €0.03/month; the error bill is ≈ €150. The error cost decides.
2. **Week 2:** measured my own extraction tool: 50% fully correct. The most dangerous failure was a gear field, not a date.
3. **Week 3:** the vendors' claims about bookings and money were all avoidances; only speed and plumbing were facts.
4. **Week 4:** put euros on it. The inquiry → quote step loses money with AI unless a human checks gear and price. Gig prep is the one step where AI pays (+€556/year from Year 2), because its errors land where I'm already checking.
