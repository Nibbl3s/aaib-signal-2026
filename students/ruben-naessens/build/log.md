# Week 2 — Build v1 test log and probe plan

## Test method

I tested the [unchanged v1 prompt](https://github.com/Nibbl3s/aaib-signal-2026/blob/main/students/ruben-naessens/build/prompt.v1.md) on ten real job ads from Multilingual Jobs Worldwide. Each run used a fresh ChatGPT chat in Instant mode, with the vacancy text and the same prompt. Expected checks were written before each run and kept out of the test chats. The linked chats contain the actual inputs and JSON outputs. The corrected Greece run is Test 01.

A vacancy passes if the reviewed output matches its expected requirements, preserves required versus preferred status and avoids out-of-scope items in the four fields. Concrete professional skills, such as coaching and negotiating, count as skills. Work conditions, eligibility conditions and general personal traits are outside the four-field schema for this evaluation. This scoring rule is a project choice.

## Baseline result

**1/10 strict passes and 9/10 vacancies with a categorisation error.** The 90% figure is the share of vacancies with at least one scored error in this sample. It does not measure the share of individual qualifications extracted incorrectly. The main recurring issue was that the model put conditions or personal traits in the skills field. It generally preserved the reviewed language levels and preferred experience. The ads came from one recruitment site, and review time was not measured.

## Three-row probe plan for v2

| Probe | Retest cases | Success criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work conditions | 01, 02, 03, 10 | Relocation, location and weekend availability stay outside the skills field. Concrete skills such as troubleshooting and cold calling are retained. |
| Personal traits versus professional skills | 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09 | Generic traits and physical conditions stay outside skills. Concrete skills such as negotiation, knife work, sourcing and coaching are retained. |
| Required versus preferred and exact thresholds | 03, 04, 05, 07, 10 | Preserve CEFR levels, experience durations and diploma status. POS, B2B/SaaS, optional education and Swedish remain preferred where specified. |

For v2, tighten the definition of technical and professional skills while retaining the same JSON schema. Re-run the ten inputs in fresh chats with the same scoring rule, then compare strict passes and the types of errors observed.
